Privacy, in plain words

What I keep. The exams and notices you chose, your alert language, and how to reach you: your email address and/or your Telegram account number. Nothing else. No names, no phone numbers, no passwords.

Why. Only to send you alerts about changes to exam notices, and sign-in links you ask for. I don't send marketing, and I don't sell or share your details.

Who else sees it. Emails go out through Resend, and Telegram messages through Telegram. Summaries of public notices are written with Anthropic's Claude; your details are never sent there. This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Stopping. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. On Telegram, send /stop all. To erase everything, sign in and use Delete everything about me: it's immediate.

Cookies. One, to keep you signed in. No trackers, no analytics.

Pehredaar is an independent project, not affiliated with SSC or any government body. Always confirm with the official notice.
